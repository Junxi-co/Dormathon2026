# Interview Guide: Solar Owners

**Goal:** Find out how real home solar owners notice problems today, what they do about them, and whether a plain-language "what to expect tomorrow and what to do" message would change a decision.

**Who to talk to:** 3-5 home solar owners (family, neighbours, friends of friends). Number interviewed so far: TO CONFIRM.

**Ground rules**
- Ask open questions. Do not describe our tool before they answer.
- Write down their exact words. Quotes are what we use in the pitch.
- Ask "tell me about the last time" instead of "would you ever".

---

## The 5 Questions

1. **Tell me about the last time your solar output looked wrong or lower than expected. How did you find out?**
2. **How do you check on your system today, and how often?** (App, bill, looking at the panels, never.)
3. **When your inverter app shows a problem, what does it tell you, and what do you do next?**
4. **Roughly how much do you think a problem like that costs you, and what would make you act (clean the panels, call the installer)?**
5. **If you got one short message the evening before saying what to expect tomorrow and what to do about it, what would it need to say for you to trust it and act?**
