# 2-Minute Pitch Script

**Total time:** 2:00. Rehearse with a timer. Practise the demo section the most.
Anything in [BRACKETS] needs real input from us. Do not say a number out loud until it is confirmed.

---

## 0:00 - 0:20 | The person and their problem (20s)

*Say:*
"Meet [NAME OR 'a homeowner we interviewed']. They have solar panels on their roof. When we asked how they find out something is wrong, they told us: **'[INSERT REAL QUOTE]'**.
Their panels can lose output for days before anyone notices, and every day costs them money."

*Presenter note:* Use the real quote from the interview notes. If we only have paraphrase, say "told us" and do not use quote marks.

---

## 0:20 - 0:35 | The gap in existing tools (15s)

*Say:*
"Inverter apps show what **already happened**. [WHAT WE SAW IN THE APP, TO CONFIRM]. They don't say what to expect tomorrow, what kind of problem it is, how much money it costs, or what to do about it."

*Presenter note:* Only claim what we actually saw in the comparison checklist (docs/interview_guide.md).

---

## 0:35 - 1:25 | Live demo: three scenario buttons (50s)

*Say:*
"There's no login and no dashboard. You press a button and get a plain-language answer."

| Time | Action | What to say |
|------|--------|-------------|
| 0:35-0:45 | Open the app and show the start screen | "This is tomorrow's forecast for a home solar system. [FORECAST, TO CONFIRM]" |
| 0:45-0:57 | Press **[SCENARIO 1 NAME]** | "[ONE-LINE RESULT: what is detected, category, money lost, what to do]" |
| 0:57-1:09 | Press **[SCENARIO 2 NAME]** | "[ONE-LINE RESULT]" |
| 1:09-1:21 | Press **[SCENARIO 3 NAME]** | "[ONE-LINE RESULT]" |
| 1:21-1:25 | Point to the ranking | "Problems are ranked by money lost, so the owner knows what to fix first." |

*Presenter note:* Run through all three buttons before going on stage. Keep a screen recording as a backup in case of Wi-Fi or app problems. Backup file location: [PATH].

---

## 1:25 - 1:50 | The proof numbers (25s)

*Say:*
"Does it help someone make a better decision? Here's the proof, from our test results:
- [METRIC 1, e.g. detection result, from results/metrics.md]
- [METRIC 2, e.g. false alarm result, from results/metrics.md]
- [IMPACT NUMBER, from docs/assumptions.md]
We tested on [DESCRIBE DATA SOURCE HONESTLY, TO CONFIRM]."

*Presenter note:* Every number must be copied from results/metrics.md or docs/assumptions.md and marked Confirmed. If the data is simulated or synthetic, say so.

---

## 1:50 - 2:00 | The close (10s)

*Say:*
"Tomorrow's Solar Plan: it tells a homeowner what will happen tomorrow, what's going wrong, what it costs, and what to do. [ONE LINE ABOUT NEXT STEP, TO CONFIRM]. Thank you."

---

## Before presenting, checklist
- [ ] Real quote inserted
- [ ] All three scenario buttons tested in the final build
- [ ] Proof numbers copied from results/metrics.md
- [ ] Impact number matches docs/assumptions.md
- [ ] Practised with a timer, finished at or under 2:00
- [ ] Backup screen recording ready
